Best States to Buy a Short-Term Rental For Sale in 2026 (What Changes Year to Year)

Published 2026-09-25 · Short Term Rental For Sale Editorial

Regulation changes faster than most rankings update

Lists ranking the best states or cities for short-term rentals go stale quickly because local and state regulation -- permit caps, minimum stay requirements, occupancy taxes -- changes far more often than the underlying demand data the rankings are usually built on.

A market that ranked well a year ago can tighten regulation and become a much harder place to operate, while a market that barely made the list can open up as regulation eases -- rankings alone won't catch either shift in time.

Evaluate current regulation and demand together, market by market

The more reliable approach is checking current permit and licensing status directly, alongside actual occupancy and ADR data for the specific submarket being considered, rather than relying on a general state-level ranking.
