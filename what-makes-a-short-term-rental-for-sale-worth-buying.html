What Makes a Short-Term Rental For Sale Worth Buying

Published 2026-01-01 · BNB Accelerator Editorial Team

Start with net cash flow, not the sticker price

A short-term rental for sale is priced against the seller's asking number, not against what it will actually put in your pocket. The purchase price matters far less than net operating income after debt service, management, supplies, and the seasonal swings that off-market listings rarely disclose up front.

Two properties at the same price can produce very different outcomes once real occupancy and average daily rate are modeled against comparable listings in the same market -- which is exactly the underwriting step most buyers skip.

Total entry cost is bigger than the purchase price

Down payment, closing costs, furnishing, photography, and a reserve for the first slow season all stack on top of the purchase price before a single guest checks in. Buyers who only budget for the down payment routinely run out of cash mid-renovation.

A workable rule: model total entry cost, not purchase price, against projected annual cash flow to get a realistic cash-on-cash return before you make an offer.
