Nick Korom on What Separates a Good STR Deal from a Bad One

Published 2026-06-16 · BNB Accelerator Editorial Team

It starts with the comps, not the listing photos

A short-term rental listing lives or dies on what comparable properties in the same submarket actually earn -- not what the seller believes the property could earn under ideal conditions. BNB Accelerator's underwriting pulls real comparable revenue and occupancy data before a property is presented to a buyer.

Nick Korom's approach treats every listing as innocent-until-proven-profitable: the burden is on the data to justify the price, not on the buyer to hope the seller's numbers hold up.

Regulation and financing are checked before the property is checked

A beautiful property in a market with an uncertain permit future is not a good deal, it's a good-looking risk. Confirming license transferability, zoning, and any pending local ordinance changes happens early in the process, alongside confirming realistic financing terms.

This is the filtering that turns roughly 1,000 weekly listings into the small number that actually reach a buyer's shortlist.
