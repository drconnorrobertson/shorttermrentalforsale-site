Best Markets to Buy a Short-Term Rental in 2026

Published 2026-01-01 · Short Term Rental For Sale Editorial

Demand, regulation, and supply -- in that order

A strong short-term rental market needs durable visitor demand first: tourism, events, proximity to outdoor recreation, or a consistent business-travel base. Regulation comes second -- a great demand market with a permit freeze or a strict owner-occupancy requirement can be effectively closed to new buyers.

Supply and competition come third: a market can have great demand and workable rules and still be saturated with inventory that compresses everyone's average daily rate.

Why market selection should come before browsing listings

Buyers who start by browsing listings nationally, rather than picking a small number of target markets first, tend to underwrite against inconsistent comparable data because they're comparing markets that don't behave the same way.
