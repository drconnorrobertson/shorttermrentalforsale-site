Turnkey vs Renovation: Which STR For Sale Fits You

Published 2026-03-07 · BNB Accelerator Editorial Team

Turnkey trades upside for speed

A genuinely turnkey short-term rental -- operational, furnished, with a booking history -- can start generating income within weeks of closing, at a price that reflects that convenience. The tradeoff is less room to force appreciation through renovation.

For a buyer who wants cash flow without a construction timeline, turnkey is usually the better fit, particularly for a first acquisition.

Renovation trades speed for a lower basis and forced equity

A property that needs work can often be acquired below the turnkey price for the same market, with the buyer building the operational history and the design from scratch. That can mean a stronger long-term cash-on-cash return, at the cost of a longer runway to first revenue and real construction risk.

This path suits buyers with either construction experience, a strong contractor relationship, or a team managing the renovation on their behalf -- which is part of what a done-for-you acquisition process coordinates when a renovation deal is the right fit.
