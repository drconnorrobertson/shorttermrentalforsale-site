Buyer evidence and purchase handoffs

Short Term Rental for Sale field guides

Practical evidence checks, worked examples and editable worksheets for buyer evidence and purchase handoffs.

6 guides

Track seller document requests by decision deadline

A buyer may request many records without knowing which unresolved item affects the next decision. A status board connects each request to the issue it addresses and the deadline that matters. Keep the board separate from contractual deadlines and have the responsible professionals confirm any legal consequences.

Identify the exact items expected to convey

Descriptions such as “furnished” can leave the buyer uncertain about the actual items included. An identifier register uses room, item, condition and a photograph reference to support the transaction discussion. The executed agreement governs what conveys; this register helps the buyer and responsible professionals identify ambiguities.

Track evidence for inspection follow-up items

An inspection report identifies findings, but the follow-up may require a specialist assessment, quote or documented repair. Track the next evidence needed for each material item. Keep the original report intact and distinguish a seller response from independent confirmation of completed work.

Plan the handoff from closing to first property access

Closing, possession and practical access may be different milestones. Confirm the agreed timing with the transaction professionals, then coordinate the operational tasks that depend on access. Keep vendor appointments conditional until the responsible parties confirm that entry is available.

Crosswalk seller revenue definitions before comparing totals

Seller statements, booking exports and bank deposits can describe different measures. A definition crosswalk records what each total includes and the period it covers. The worksheet helps the buyer reconcile the evidence without treating a headline booking number as verified owner income.

Hand off unresolved purchase items to the design team

Some purchase questions affect furnishing decisions even after the buyer has selected a property. Record retained inventory uncertainty, access constraints and work that must precede installation. The handoff prevents design from approving orders based on assumptions that the acquisition team has not confirmed.
