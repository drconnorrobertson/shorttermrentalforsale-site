Buyer evidence and purchase handoffs · Field guide · October 9, 2026

Plan the handoff from closing to first property access

Closing, possession and practical access may be different milestones. Confirm the agreed timing with the transaction professionals, then coordinate the operational tasks that depend on access. Keep vendor appointments conditional until the responsible parties confirm that entry is available.

Work through the decision

  1. Confirm the agreed access milestone.
  2. Identify keys and access systems to be handed over.
  3. Schedule the first condition review.
  4. Notify vendors when access is confirmed.

For each action, name the person responsible for preparing the response and the person who approves it. Record what evidence would close the item. If new information changes cost, timing or scope, update the affected plan and identify who needs the revised version.

Common failure to avoid

Do not infer permission to enter from a tentative closing date or an informal message. Coordinate through the responsible transaction parties.

Worked example

In an illustrative purchase, delivery was planned for the morning after closing but access timing remains unconfirmed. The coordinator keeps the delivery conditional and confirms the handover before the carrier is dispatched.

Close the handoff

A finished worksheet should identify the result, its evidence and any remaining owner. If the result is uncertain, record the next action instead of marking the whole task complete. Keep the worksheet with the relevant source file and tell the receiving person which version applies.
