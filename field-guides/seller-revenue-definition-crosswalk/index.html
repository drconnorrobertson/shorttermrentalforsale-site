Buyer evidence and purchase handoffs · Field guide · October 9, 2026

Crosswalk seller revenue definitions before comparing totals

Seller statements, booking exports and bank deposits can describe different measures. A definition crosswalk records what each total includes and the period it covers. The worksheet helps the buyer reconcile the evidence without treating a headline booking number as verified owner income.

Evidence to assemble

Gather the source records before completing the worksheet. A missing record should remain an open question. Keep the original alongside any summary so the next person can trace the decision.

Work through the decision

  1. Record period and accounting basis for each source.
  2. Separate accommodation charges from other amounts.
  3. Identify refunds and deductions.
  4. Explain timing differences before comparing totals.

For each action, name the person responsible for preparing the response and the person who approves it. Record what evidence would close the item. If new information changes cost, timing or scope, update the affected plan and identify who needs the revised version.

Common failure to avoid

Do not force bank deposits to equal booking revenue by adding unexplained adjustments. Preserve the difference and request the missing evidence.

Worked example

A hypothetical booking export includes guest charges before refunds, while a payout report reflects fees and payment timing. The crosswalk documents those definitions before the buyer evaluates any annual total.

Close the handoff

A finished worksheet should identify the result, its evidence and any remaining owner. If the result is uncertain, record the next action instead of marking the whole task complete. Keep the worksheet with the relevant source file and tell the receiving person which version applies.
