Done-For-You STR Acquisition: How BNB Accelerator Works

Published 2026-05-13 · BNB Accelerator Editorial Team

Sourcing and underwriting happen before you see anything

BNB Accelerator's acquisitions team screens more than 1,000 short-term rental listings a week across eight states. Roughly 98% are eliminated at the sourcing stage on price, condition, or market fit before underwriting even starts.

What survives gets modeled line by line against actual comparable revenue and occupancy data -- the same standard applied whether the listing came from the team's own pipeline or from a buyer's own find.

Financing, closing and setup are coordinated, not handed off

Once a property is selected, the team works through financing structure, closing timeline, and furnishing and design so the property can go live quickly rather than sitting vacant for months post-closing.

Tax strategy -- cost segregation, bonus depreciation, and the 7-day rule around material participation -- is modeled in partnership with AE Tax Advisors alongside the acquisition, not bolted on afterward.
