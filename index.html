The buyer's desk

Read the deal in the right order.

Premium acquisition work is not louder marketing. It is a disciplined sequence that makes assumptions, tradeoffs and unresolved questions easy to see before an offer is written.

01 / Market

Start with permission and demand

Confirm local rules, realistic demand drivers and seasonal behavior before analyzing a specific house.

02 / Asset

Price the entire launch

Model acquisition, repair, furnishing, insurance, reserves and operating setup as one capital decision.

03 / Decision

Compare base and downside cases

Use ranges, not a single optimistic forecast, and let financing terms and exit options shape the final call.

Acquisition path

From listing discovery to an informed offer.

  1. Define the buy box.
    Budget, market, property profile, return objective and operating involvement.
  2. Validate the location.
    Regulation, seasonality, supply, access and durable guest demand.
  3. Evaluate the property.
    Comparable performance, total entry cost, operating burden and reserves.
  4. Coordinate diligence.
    Inspection, insurance, financing, title and advice from licensed professionals.
  5. Plan the launch.
    Design, furnishing, photography, pricing and local operations before closing.
