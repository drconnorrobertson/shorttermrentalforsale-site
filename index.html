Why buyers use us

Buying a short-term rental for sale is a underwriting problem, not a browsing problem

We underwrite before you tour

Every listing is checked against actual comparable revenue data, occupancy and ADR for its specific market before it ever reaches you -- not the seller's optimistic pro forma.

We handle financing and structure

DSCR loans, conventional debt, entity structure and closing timeline -- mapped out before you make an offer, so there are no surprises at the table.

We coordinate the tax strategy

Cost segregation, bonus depreciation and material participation are modeled alongside the deal, in partnership with AE Tax Advisors, so the purchase and the tax plan move together.

The process

From "show me what's for sale" to keys in hand

  • Screen. We source short-term rental listings across eight states and eliminate roughly 98% before you see them.
  • Underwrite. Surviving listings get a full revenue and expense model against real comparable data.
  • Present. You review a small, vetted shortlist with purchase price, entry cost, projected cash flow and cash-on-cash return.
  • Acquire. Financing, closing and design/furnishing are coordinated so the property is guest-ready fast.
  • Operate. Choose self-management, co-hosting or full management once the property is live.

Who this is for

High-income W-2 earners and business owners who want a cash-flowing short-term rental without spending a year learning underwriting, regulations and revenue management first.
